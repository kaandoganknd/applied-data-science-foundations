# CardioGood Fitness Customer Profiles

This SPSS project profiles the customers who purchased the TM195, TM498, and TM798 treadmill models. The analysis uses 180 purchase records and compares customer demographics, household income, planned usage, expected mileage, and self-rated fitness.

## Questions

- What is the typical customer profile for each product line?
- Which customer characteristics differ across product lines?
- Are product choice, gender, relationship status, and self-rated fitness associated?

## Main findings

- **TM195** is the highest-volume line. Its customers have the lowest mean income, report moderate planned use, and have a mean fitness score close to 3 out of 5.
- **TM498** has a profile close to TM195. The two lines have similar mean age, education, fitness, intended use, and expected mileage.
- **TM798** is the premium segment. Its buyers have higher mean income and education, plan more weekly use and mileage, and report higher fitness. The product is also strongly male-skewed in this sample.

The project reports associations, not causal effects. The data cannot show that any customer characteristic causes product selection.

## Repository contents

- `analysis-report.md` — concise findings and test interpretations.
